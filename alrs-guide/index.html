ALRS Web App

The Automated Lodging Reservation System (ALRS) by Howard Day and Associates is how you book UTA lodging.

Open alrsweb.com

Welcome to the ALRS Web

The ALRS Web can be accessed on your phone or on your PC.

To access the site, go to https://alrsweb.com.

For first time users, click on REGISTER HERE to register your email.

Create a New Account

You will need your mobile (or member) ID. A mobile ID should be sent to you via SMS text. After you enter your member ID, click Next.

If this is the first time you are logging into the ALRS, your mobile ID will be your 10-digit phone number. Enter your 10-digit phone number without the dashes and click Verify.

Note: If you have previously logged into the ALRS, use your personal PIN.

Once your mobile ID and PIN are verified, register with your login email address. This email will be used by the system to send you reservation and cancellation information.

After clicking Submit, the system will send a verification email to your registered address. Open the email titled "Confirm Email" and click Click here to Confirm.

Enter and confirm your new password, then click REGISTER.

Password rules: 6–100 characters, with at least one digit, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one non-alphanumeric character.

Logging In

Go to https://alrsweb.com, enter your web login email and password, and click LOG IN.

The system will send a one-time security code to your email and/or phone via SMS text. Enter the 6-digit security code, then click CONTINUE.

Reserve

Reservations must be made before the CUT-OFF date for each scheduled drill. The cut-off date and time is shown in the Cut Off column to the right of each published UTA's Arrive and Depart dates.

Select your arrival and departure dates by tapping the ARRIVE and AM Depart fields, or select dates from the published UTA schedule. Click NEXT.

Click RESERVE to make your reservation for the drill.

A confirmation will be sent with reservation details to your email. You can also see your reservation details on the web under the Bookings tab.

Bookings

Scroll through your bookings to display booking confirmation details. From the Bookings tab you can:

  • Call the hotel
  • Use the Google Maps feature to map driving directions
  • Cancel reservations
  • Check on reservation status

Cancellations

You can cancel an individual night or the entire reservation with all the nights.

To cancel an individual night, click the red trash can to the right of the reservation date.

To cancel all nights, click the trash can to the left of the range of reservation nights.

Click YES to confirm the cancellation.
