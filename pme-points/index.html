Points Credit for Correspondence PME as a Reservist

In-residence: ARC members receive active duty points while attending in-residence courses on orders.

Non-resident: ARC members may receive DL (formerly ECI) points for completing certain non-resident distributed learning courses used to meet training requirements related to unit readiness or mobilization. To get DL points for a completed course:

  • Must be RSSB approved
  • Must have completed the AF equivalent first
  • Total DL hours must be listed in the course syllabus
  • Must have proof of satisfactory completion
  • Qualifying courses: AU courses, AF PME, CDCs
  • May qualify if additional requirements are met: FEMA, DAU, Cyber, non-Air Force PME
  • Non-qualifying courses: JKO (other than SEJPME), ADLS, CCAF

Points Not Showing Up? Request a Correction

Points normally update automatically within two months of course completion. If they don't, submit a correction request:

  1. Log into myFSS: myfss.us.af.mil (or via MyFSS on the AF Portal).
  2. Search for the article "AFR/ANG General Point Credit Information".
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Create A Request.
  4. Select Category, then Retirement Point.
  5. Attach the Verification of Completion from the AU portal. It must show course study hours, course title, course code, pass/fail status, and completion date.

Getting your Verification of Completion:

  • Completed AU courses through Arizona State University? Get it from au.asu.edu.
  • Air University students: AU Portal → Distance Learning → hover over AUSIS → ALS, NCOA, SNCO, ACSC, SOS etc. → AUSIS → Study Hours. You can then download your Verification of Completion.

Check Your Points in vMPF

In vMPF go to Personal Data → ANG/USAFR Point Credit Summary Inquiry (PCARS).

  • Point Credit Summary: points you earned show up under the ADL column.
  • Advanced Distributed Learning: individual classes show up here.
