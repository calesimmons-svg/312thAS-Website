Reserve Pay

Starting with the most important topic: the ways to pay yourself and coding for FAs/781s. There are 3 primary ways.

NOTE: Never start any appointment with "I'm a reservist…" — nothing good will come from it. Sshhh. Back to pay…

1 – AT / RPA / MPA (one-day period)

  • Considered "active duty"; cannot use UTAs or TPs in conjunction
  • If you have a David Grant appointment, being on a 1 can be helpful
  • 15 days of AT per year — USE them, please; giving them back is not ideal
  • MPA is AMC-allocated funding for missions
  • 3 MPA vacancies for an augmented crew per mission
  • RPA is an extra status if you use all AT and forecast to use all IDT statuses

AT

  • Requires 10 days advance request
  • Case-by-case. Likely approved for OSTs and missions with justification
  • Not for your regular everyday reservist things

2 – UTA (Unit Training Assembly) – 4-hr period

  • Inactive duty, aka IDT
  • May perform 2 per day
  • 48 per year / max 16 per quarter
  • 40A (UTA Reschedule form) submission required for pay

3 – GTPs / FTPs (TEEPs) – 4-hr period

  • Bonus statuses we get for being aircrew
  • Inactive duty, aka IDT
  • Flying TPs: max 2 per day (72 max per FY)
    • Up to 72 FTPs per year / max 18 per quarter
    • Sim flying requirements to log: 1 hr for a single, 3 hrs total for double FTPs
    • Airplane flying requirements to log: .1 hr each FTP; .2 hrs total for double FTPs
    • Can have 100% be FTPs
  • GTP: max 1 per day
    • 36 GTPs per year / max 18 per quarter (can't go over 72 with FTPs)
    • At least half of your TPs must be FTPs
    • TP Binder entry required for pay
  • Extraneous – RMPs ("RUMPS")
    • Usually reserved for CCs/DOs

4 – IDTR (using GTPs/UTAs with a travel voucher)

  • Email an IDT travel request to scheduling 10 days in advance.
  • Do NOT travel without notice that IDT travel is authorized.

**Requires AFRC approval**

A Good Year

You have to make 50 points to have a "good year." If you don't have a good year, that year won't count toward your retirement — and you don't want to find that out when you go to retire.

Point score

  • 15 points for breathing (congratulations!)
  • 1 point per AT/RPA/MPA day
  • 1 point per UTA period
  • 1 point per TP period

Busy year? No sweat. To get that good year, use all your UTAs (48 + 15 for breathing = good year). You're not required to use a single TP.

Gaming Your Statuses (Pro Tips and Technique Only)

Late-night sims — I would use a 1/AT.

  • 4th-period sim from 1800–0200 (touches the next day): you get 3 days of AT vs 2.
  • If life has led you to neglect TPs, go for TPs.

Locals and any flying events — we recommend FTPs (flying TPs/33s).

  • This gives you flexibility to log more GTPs later.
  • You can use all 72 TPs as FTPs, but only half as GTPs.
  • If you have an extra duty in the squadron, GTPs are gold.

Participation Expectations

  • We don't expect you at drill weekends as long as you are current.
  • If you're notified that you're overdue on anything, it's not an automatic "attend the next drill weekend." Clarify with your flight CC. Schedulers will send you bits of info all the time, and the 349th's priorities change month to month. Don't burn out — just ask more questions.
  • I charge you with flying (or attempting to fly) 2 missions/OSTs a year. It's the only way to keep the whole unit current. Always open to feedback. If locals are the contribution your life can provide, that's OK. Stay off the 90-day no-fly list.

Scheduling

We're here to help, and we know being a reservist is confusing. Send all inquiries to 312.as.pilots@us.af.mil · 707-424-5354.

We try our best to respond to all emails in a timely fashion and reply once your inquiry is completed. One scheduler may be talking with you one day and be on a mission or leave the next. If it's not time sensitive, the org box is the best point of contact.

If you are planning to fly a local:

  • Double flying TPs (no advance request required).
  • BUT if the local cancels:
    • Without prior coordination, you're limited to logging a G1.
    • With advance coordination, possibly a U1G1. It may be worth making the request even if you don't end up needing it.
    • Call your schedulers with further questions or creative solutions.
