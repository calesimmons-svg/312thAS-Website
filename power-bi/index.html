Mission Readiness at a Glance: 312AS Power BI

Power BI lets members of the 312th Airlift Squadron review all currencies, training, and accomplishments to verify Go/No-Go status before your next C-5M sortie. It works on both your iPad and your desktop.

Currency Viewer (Power BI) Guide

Choose Your Pre-Flight Path

Getting to the 312AS Currency Report is a little different depending on your device. The iPad steps come first, then the desktop steps.

iPad Setup: App Installation

  1. Locate and open the Hub app on your squadron iPad.
  2. Search for and download Microsoft Power BI.

iPad Setup: Locating Your Currency

  1. Open Power BI. The 312AS Currency Report will often appear automatically under your Recent tab.
  2. If it doesn't: tap the Shared tab at the bottom, select the 312AS Workspace, and tap the 312AS Currency Report.

Desktop Setup: Access via MS Teams

  1. Open Microsoft Teams on your desktop.
  2. In the left-hand rail menu, click Apps.
  3. Search for, download, and open the Power BI app inside Teams.

Desktop Setup: Locating the Report

  1. Inside the Power BI app in Teams, expand the left-hand Browse menu.
  2. Click Shared with me.
  3. Select the 312AS Currency Report from the list.

Your Flightline Go/No-Go Status

The Currency Report turns your training data into one dashboard. Check your availability code, track upcoming due dates, and see any overdue items grounding you.

  • Primary Readiness: your immediate flight clearance status.
  • Immediate Action Required: grounding items that must be resolved before your next C-5M sortie.
  • Lapsed Currencies: specific tasks requiring requalification.
