How to refund your positive GTC card balance

If you used your personal card by accident or by necessity on a trip it will likely leave your GTC with a positive balance. Here's how to refund that to your bank account.

  • Log in: home.cards.citidirect.com/CommercialCard/login
  • Under the teal tab, hit Request Refund.

Put the refund amount, and you're good to go!

CZTE – Combat Zone Tax Exemption (Form 1881)

Tax free all month. Follow these steps to submit your paperwork with finance.

Step 1 – Go to AROWS-R and download all orders for the trip, and any school or seasoning orders: arowsr.afrc.af.mil

Download the 1881 at the CSP site under documents. Try these links: CSP Documents · CSP Documents home

Go to DTS, find your authorization that matches the trip and print it (see below), then save that PDF for later.

Go to DTS, find your voucher that matches the trip and print it (see below), then save that PDF for later.

Then go to DTS, find the last page and screenshot where you signed it (see below). Use the Snipping Tool if you want.

AF Form 1881 (blank)

Then download the 1881 form and fill it out to match the example. For each person, when they submit it, line up the social on the left for yourself for the submittal. You might be able to do one form with just the last 4 per person — I have heard this works too.

1881 example / folder (Google Drive)
Shared drive: U:\04 - Additional Duty (PA)\16. DTS_ODTA (PA)\Info Guides

Then to submit it, go to this site (worked for me in Chrome, might have to log in): SAF/FM CSP Portal

Click Create Inquiry. It should pre-populate with all your info — give it a little bit. If not, fill it all out on the left side, then make the right side look like mine.

Here's that text if you want to copy it: 

Hello team, attached is my...
-AF 1881 signed by the Aircraft Commander
-DTS travel voucher
-DTS authorization
-DTS signature page
-Orders + mods.
Please let me know if you need any more information.

Then go to the bottom and attach your travel orders + mods, the 1881 form, and the PDFs from DTS above.

How to generate a NONA from DTS

1. Go to your Authorization (not voucher). If you already signed it, you're too late to do this.

2. Go to Review Reservations → Add to Trip → Add New Lodging.

3. Hit Search — it will generate a CNA number like the one below. Screenshot the whole thing; it will suffice for DTS. It sometimes gives you an actual PDF to download.

Cell Phone International Fees Reimbursement

Things you need to gather (I did a month-long reimbursement, which was 2 trips):

  • Orders from trip / school orders / long tour orders you may be on for said charges
  • Cell phone bill showing overages (T-Mobile is very specific if you look around on it)
  • Signed memo from our AO
  • This form: OF 1164 – Claim for Reimbursement for Expenditures on Official Business
